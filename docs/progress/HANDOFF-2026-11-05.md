## 3. What works (verify in under a minute)

- **Suggested comps:** `/w/<slug>/properties/<id>/analysis` → Comps card → **Suggested comps**.
  - Each suggestion shows its distance or "Same ZIP", a match score out of 100, and its source and as-of date.
  - **Add** or **Add all** saves the comp, and the median $/unit updates.
  - The list fills once other properties have a sourced sale price and date, comps are saved elsewhere, or a RentCast estimate has been fetched.
- **Spanish follow-up steps:** Outreach → Sequences → a step → **Add Spanish version**.
  - On a contact whose language is Spanish, the due step shows "Español" with the Spanish text. **Use English / Usar español** switches between versions.
  - If a step has no Spanish version, it says "Prefers Spanish, but this step has no Spanish version."
- **Spanish HELP reply:** Settings → Integrations → Texting → "Reply to AYUDA, in Spanish".
- **Inbound Spanish keywords** (once texting is on): ALTO, PARA, PARAR, DETENER, CANCELAR and BAJA work like STOP; INICIAR and REANUDAR like START; AYUDA like HELP. A sentence like "Para mañana está bien" stays an ordinary text.

## 9. Decisions (ADRs)

- **ADR-042, automatic comps:**
  - suggestions come from data already in the workspace and are added by a person;
  - the server recomputes suggestions by key, so a saved comp always carries its source's numbers;
  - RentCast comparables are labeled "list price, not a recorded sale".
- **ADR-043, Spanish templates:**
  - a Spanish version per step, chosen by `contacts.language`;
  - Spanish STOP, START and HELP words are matched on the whole message only;
  - the Spanish HELP reply falls back to English.
- **Would reverse:** nothing. "PARA" as a stop word is deliberate (CTIA-style whole-message match), but watch for people who reply just "para" meaning something else.

## 10. Known issues / tech debt (ranked)

1. Comp suggestions read up to 2,000 recorded sales and 2,000 saved comps per page view. That's fine at today's volumes, but they would need an index or a materialized view at large scale.
2. Saved comps from other properties have no coordinates, so they rank by ZIP or city only.
3. Newsletters, the booking page and the HELP-reply defaults are still English only. They can reuse the same pattern.
4. CI's `format:check` caught one unformatted file on #32 (fixed). Run `pnpm format:check` before pushing; it isn't part of `pnpm lint`.
